Collaboration Policies

Please review our collaboration guidelines before applying

1 Content Guidelines

  • Content must be authentic and align with your personal brand
  • High-quality photos and videos showcasing our dishes
  • Honest reviews and genuine recommendations
  • Proper disclosure of sponsored content as per platform guidelines

2 Engagement Requirements

  • Open to influencers of all sizes - no minimum follower requirement
  • Active engagement with your audience
  • Content focused on food, lifestyle, or related niches
  • Passion for sharing authentic Pakistani cuisine

3 Compensation & Benefits

  • Complimentary meals for content creation
  • Payment per video posted (not long-term contract)
  • Special discounts for future visits
  • Early access to new menu items and special events

4 Flexible Agreement Terms

  • Simple per-video agreement - create 3-4 videos only
  • No long-term commitment required
  • Content approval before posting to ensure quality
  • Option to continue collaboration if both parties agree
