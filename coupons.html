Earn Free Meals

Join our loyalty program and get rewarded for your love of great food

How to Claim Your Free Meal Coupon

At Spice Haven, we believe in rewarding our loyal customers. That's why we've created an exciting loyalty program that lets you earn free meals simply by enjoying the food you love!

The Challenge

Complete our monthly challenge and receive a coupon for a completely free meal of your choice from our menu. Here's how it works:

Challenge Requirements

  • Order 10 meals within a single calendar month
  • Your total spending must exceed Rs 9,999 for those 10 meals combined
  • All orders must be placed under the same registered phone number

How to Get Started

  1. 1 Register your phone number with us during your first order. Simply provide your mobile number when placing an order at our restaurant or mention it when ordering online.
  2. 2 Track your progress by asking our staff or using our chatbot on this website. We'll keep count of your orders and total spending automatically.
  3. 3 Complete the challenge by reaching 10 meals worth more than Rs 9,999 within the same month.
  4. 4 Receive your coupon via SMS at the end of the month. Your coupon will be valid for 30 days and can be redeemed for any dish on our menu!

What You Can Get

Your free meal coupon can be redeemed for any single dish from our menu, including our premium items like Mutton Karahi, Beef Nihari, or our signature Chicken Biryani. The choice is entirely yours!

Pro Tip

If you're a regular customer who visits us weekly, you can easily complete this challenge! Just 2-3 visits per week with an average order of Rs 1,000-1,200 will get you there. Plus, you can combine this with our discounted items to maximize your savings!

Terms & Conditions

  • • Challenge resets on the 1st of each month
  • • Only dine-in and takeaway orders count (delivery orders excluded)
  • • Coupons cannot be combined with other offers or discounts
  • • Coupons are non-transferable and must be used by the registered customer
  • • One coupon per customer per month
  • • Management reserves the right to modify or discontinue the program at any time

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I share my coupon with family or friends?

No, coupons are non-transferable and can only be used by the registered customer who earned them.

Do discounted items count toward the Rs 9,999 total?

Yes! The amount you actually pay (after discounts) counts toward your total. This makes it even easier to reach the goal.

What happens if I complete the challenge multiple times in one month?

You'll receive one coupon per month. However, your orders will continue to count toward the next month's challenge!

How do I check my progress?

Simply ask our staff during your visit, call us, or use our chatbot on this website to check your current order count and spending total.
